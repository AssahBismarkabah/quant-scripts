# Phase A Primary-Source Evidence

This record contains the sources used to establish rules. It deliberately does not treat educational commentary or a historical return as proof of an edge.

| Source | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [CME Final Settlement Procedures](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/settlement.html) | S&P 500 equity-index futures/options use a Special Opening Quotation based on component-stock opening prices; expiring contracts are cash settled. | A pre-trade buy/sell direction, participant-level obligation, or retail execution advantage. |
| [CME Rule 363](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/363/363.pdf) | The final settlement quotation and expiration convention for the S&P 500 contract. | That the settlement process leaves a predictable exploitable residual after institutional hedging. |
| [CME TMAC](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/tmac-on-equity-index-futures.html) | A marker-price execution facility exists for clients managing settlement risk. | That marker users create a one-sided forced flow available to a retail trader. |
| [SEC ETF prospectus example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1100663/000119312525211179/d34352d485apos.htm) | Authorized participants, creation units, baskets, cutoffs, and primary-market mechanics. | The AP's intended basket transaction before execution or retail access to the primary mechanism. |
| [Treasury auction FAQ](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/help-center/faqs/auction-faqs/) | Direct and indirect bidder categories, auction participation, and payment/delivery requirements. | A known directional imbalance before the auction or a guaranteed secondary-market reaction. |
| [Treasury auction rules](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/laws-and-regulations/auction-regulations-uoc/) | The Uniform Offering Circular and legal auction framework. | A retail-scale execution advantage. |
| [Primary-dealer criteria](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/files/laws-and-regulations/gsa/gsa-reg-gsr-92-rpt.pdf) | Primary dealers are expected to participate meaningfully in auctions and make markets. | A requirement to take a particular directional position at a particular time. |
| [Treasury buyback FAQ](https://treasurydirect.gov/help-center/faqs/buyback-faqs/) | Buyback eligibility, FedTrade process, and delivery responsibilities. | Pre-trade visibility into accepted offers or a retail-accessible order book. |
| [OCC cash-settled currency options](https://www.theocc.com/clearance-and-settlement/clearing/u-s-dollar-cash-settled-currency-options) | Exercise settlement value and cash delivery timing. | Directional pressure that can be known before expiration. |
| [S&P index methodology example](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/methodology/article/dow-jones-bic-50-index-methodology/) | Scheduled announcements and effective dates for index changes. | A new mechanism distinct from the already closed index-rebalancing work. |

## Source-quality rule

Where a source provides only a legal or operational rule, this audit records only that rule. It does not infer a probability, expected return, price effect, or profitable trade from the rule.
