# Phase A4 Primary-Source Evidence

This record states what official sources establish and what they do not establish. No source is treated as evidence of profitability.

| Source | Establishes | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [BVMAC investor access](https://www.bvm-ac.org/espace-investisseurs-fr/investisseurs-acces-aux-produits-boursiers/) | Individual access occurs through a licensed Société de Bourse and securities/cash accounts. | Direct API access, simultaneous multi-broker execution, or guaranteed fills. |
| [BVMAC general rules](https://www.bvm-ac.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Reglement-BVMAC.pdf) | Cash-only and fixing-related market rules, transaction publication, and suspension procedures. | A repeatable executable discrepancy or reliable exit capacity. |
| [BVMAC feasibility audit](../../IA/bvmac-cemac-feasibility.md) | Existing primary-source review found weak liquidity, sparse prints, broker-mediated execution, and material published tariff components. | That every broker has identical terms or that a future broker agreement could never change the facts. |
| [BEAC CEMAC public-securities market](https://www.beac.int/m-des-titres-publics/presentation-generale/) | Primary issuance is reserved for SVTs; secondary dealers display buy/sell quotes. | Our access to SVT inventory/allocation or a cross-market executable spread. |
| [Uniswap fees](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/get-started/concepts/fees) | Swaps charge pool/protocol fees; fee tiers and active liquidity matter. | Net arbitrage after other venue fees, gas, price impact, and transfer costs. |
| [Uniswap swap execution](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/get-started/concepts/traders/swaps) | AMM execution has price impact, slippage, gas, transaction deadlines, and possible reversion. | A persistent discrepancy, funded wallet route, or positive economics for this project. |

## Evidence rule

Operational friction is not automatically an edge. It becomes a candidate only when both sides can be funded, observed, executed, settled, and unwound under a conservative complete-cost model. No such candidate was established in A4.
