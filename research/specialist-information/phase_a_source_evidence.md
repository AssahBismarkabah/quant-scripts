# Phase A3 Primary-Source Evidence

This evidence record separates public specialist material from genuinely private or restricted information. It does not infer a trading return from any source.

| Source | Establishes | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [BVMAC official site](https://www.bvm-ac.org/) | BVMAC publishes official bulletins, notices, market information, issuer admissions, and trading materials. | That the information is exclusive to us or produces a predictable price direction. |
| [BVMAC general rules](https://www.bvm-ac.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/LE-REGLEMENT-GENERAL-DE-LA-BVMAC.pdf) | BVMAC publicizes transactions through the Bulletin Officiel de la Cote and can suspend or change trading procedures by notice. | Advance private access, a forced counterparty, or executable alpha during suspension/notice events. |
| [BEAC public-securities market description](https://www.beac.int/m-des-titres-publics/presentation-generale/) | CEMAC public securities have primary and secondary markets; primary issuance is reserved for SVTs, which have client-placement obligations. | That we are an SVT, that we see allocations before publication, or that auction information creates a tradeable residual. |
| [BEAC announcements and communiqués](https://www.beac.int/m-des-titres-publics/annonces-et-communiques/) | BEAC publishes auction announcements, results, calendars, and market reports. | Exclusive timing, private order flow, or a known directional trade. |
| [BEAC Treasury statistics](https://www.beac.int/m-des-titres-publics/statistiques-mensuelles-marche-valeurs-tresor/) | Monthly CEMAC Treasury-market statistics are published. | Intraday or participant-level information before repricing. |

## Evidence rule

“Specialist” means more than local language, local geography, or difficulty finding a public webpage. It requires a specific lawful information advantage with a timestamp, an affected instrument, and a reproducible access path. No such path was established for this project during A3.
