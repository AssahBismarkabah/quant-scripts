# Phase A2 Primary-Source Evidence

This record distinguishes what official sources prove from what they do not prove. No source below is treated as evidence of a return, probability, or profitable strategy.

| Primary source | Establishes | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [SEC Investment Company Liquidity Risk Management Rules](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-company-liquidity-risk-management-program-rules) | Open-end funds classify liquidity, maintain liquidity programs, and generally cannot acquire illiquid assets above the 15% limit. | Public fund-level trade direction, timing, or forced selling signal. |
| [SEC Liquidity Risk FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/investment-company-liquidity-risk-management-programs-frequently-asked-questions) | Funds may use market depth, trading characteristics, and internal procedures in liquidity classifications. | A public, timestamped classification feed usable before a trade. |
| [FTSE Russell US Indexes Methodology](https://research.ftserussell.com/products/downloads/Russell-US-indexes.pdf) | Russell applies exchange, price, market-capitalization, and float eligibility rules. | A new mechanism distinct from closed index-eligibility/rebalance work. |
| [Nasdaq Initial Listing Guide](https://listingcenter.nasdaq.com/Assets/Initialguide.pdf) | Nasdaq listing standards include public-float, shareholder, price, and market-maker requirements. | A recurring forced secondary-market trade or directional pressure. |
| [FINRA Notice 93-54](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/93-54) | Certain OTC market makers must honor displayed minimum quotation sizes. | A buy/sell direction, an adverse counterparty, or a retail advantage. |
| [SEC Regulation SHO FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/trading-markets-frequently-asked-questions-8) | Most equity short sales require a locate; threshold-security fails have close-out rules. | A predictable long signal, public borrow availability, or execution at acceptable cost. |
| [SEC Short Sales final rule](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2004/07/short-sales) | Regulation SHO establishes locate, marking, and delivery requirements. | That hard-to-borrow status itself creates a profitable and executable trade. |
| Issuer prospectuses and lockup agreements | A particular IPO or placement may restrict resale until a specified date. | That holders will sell at the unlock, the amount sold, or a stable cross-event mechanism. |
| SEC Rule 144 materials | Restricted holders face resale conditions and volume/manner limitations. | A forced sale or public pre-trade intention. |
| ETF prospectus sources already recorded in [A1 evidence](../forced-flow-settlement/phase_a_source_evidence.md) | AP-only creation/redemption and basket mechanics. | Retail access to the primary mechanism; this is a duplicate of A1. |

## Evidence rule

A regulatory capacity constraint is necessary but not sufficient. The audit requires a pre-trade observable, a pressure direction, an identifiable counterparty, and executable economics. Where those are absent, the candidate is rejected or marked unverifiable rather than promoted to a trading test.
