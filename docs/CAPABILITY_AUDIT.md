## Cameroon/CEMAC Proximity — The Only Profile-Specific Possibility

Your Cameroon location is the only profile-specific possibility I can see from what you have shared—but it must be treated skeptically.

- **BVMAC** is based in Douala and is a regional CEMAC exchange.
- Local business knowledge could in principle matter.
- **But:** its displayed official session had very small turnover and several listed equities with no trades, which strongly suggests it is unsuitable for active or intraday trading at meaningful size.
- This is an inference to verify with a licensed local broker and actual order-book data, **not a recommendation to trade it**.

**The potential opportunity is NOT "trade Cameroon stocks because they are illiquid."** Wide spreads and poor exits are costs, not an edge.

It would be:

```text
local / specialized knowledge
→ a repeatable public observable
→ a specific slow or constrained counterparty
→ a legally tradeable instrument
→ net positive results after brutal liquidity costs
```

For example, a real capability could be building a time-stamped dataset around a narrow regional business process that global funds do not collect or understand well. But the raw information must be legal and public, the effect must happen often enough to test, and you must be able to enter and exit without giving the edge back in spread and impact.

## The Four Concrete Audit Questions

If all four cannot be answered for a domain, it is not a candidate. It does not matter how elegant the code is.

1. **What information** can you access or understand earlier, more completely, or more accurately than the marginal global investor—through language, geography, industry knowledge, or software collection?

2. **Is there a tradeable market** connected to that information?

3. **Why would the other side trade despite the information?** A mandate, slow process, illiquidity, lack of coverage, hedging need, or operational constraint must exist.

4. **Can the advantage survive costs, sparse data, legal restrictions, and small sample size?**
